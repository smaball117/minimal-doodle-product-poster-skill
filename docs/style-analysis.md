# Style Analysis

## 1. Style definition

This visual system is best described as:

**Minimal Product Photography + Doodle Micro-Storytelling**

It combines premium commercial product photography with sparse black doodles that turn a single product into a miniature world. The key is not “cute illustration”. The key is contrast:

- photography is clean, realistic, and polished;
- doodles are loose, naive, and spontaneous;
- composition is restrained and spacious;
- storytelling is functional rather than decorative.

## 2. Visual DNA

### 2.1 Large negative space

Typical behavior:

- white background dominates the frame;
- roughly 70%–80% of the poster remains visually quiet;
- the subject is centered lower in the composition;
- upper-left or left-side whitespace is reserved for a short handwritten phrase and arrow.

The whitespace is an active design element. It creates premium calm and lets the tiny doodle world feel more surprising.

### 2.2 One photographic hero subject

The hero subject is usually one object:

- fruit;
- a drink;
- ice;
- a dessert;
- another simple object with a clear sensory feature.

It must feel photographed, not illustrated:

- clean studio light;
- soft contact shadow;
- high-key exposure;
- visible texture;
- material realism;
- no environment-heavy scene.

### 2.3 Black hand-drawn doodles

The doodle system is intentionally imperfect:

- black single lines;
- thin or medium pen weight;
- slightly unstable contours;
- very simple body shapes;
- almost no facial detail;
- no filled colors;
- no polished vector finish.

The desired feeling is “someone casually added notes on top of a premium product photo”.

### 2.4 Miniature workers, not random mascots

This is the most important storytelling rule.

Every miniature person should have a job tied to the product.

Examples:

| Product feature | Visual metaphor | Miniature action |
|---|---|---|
| hydration | water supply | worker sprays water with a hose |
| sweetness | quality inspection | worker checks sweetness |
| seeds | cargo | worker transports seeds in a cart |
| cooling | climate control | worker operates a fan |
| freshness | maintenance | worker polishes or inspects surface |
| fizz | energy | workers manage bubbles or add ice |
| softness | surface care | worker brushes or checks texture |

The micro-story becomes convincing when the workers behave as if the product is a huge landscape, building, machine, pool, or worksite.

### 2.5 Short handwritten caption

Caption behavior:

- 2–6 Chinese characters is ideal;
- casual and observational;
- not a formal slogan;
- usually upper-left or left side;
- paired with one hand-drawn curved arrow.

Examples of suitable tone:

- 补水中
- 请勿打扰
- 今天有点甜
- 正在降温
- 清爽营业中

The caption should unlock the visual joke or emotion, not explain the whole product.

## 3. Composition model

A useful default vertical layout:

```text
┌──────────────────────┐
│                      │
│   handwritten text   │
│        ↘             │
│                      │
│          doodle      │
│                      │
│       HERO SUBJECT   │
│      + workers       │
│                      │
│          shadow      │
└──────────────────────┘
```

Recommended proportions:

- background whitespace: 70%–80%;
- hero subject: approximately 30%–45% of canvas height;
- doodles: visually secondary to the subject;
- text block: small and isolated;
- only one directional arrow.

Do not distribute doodles evenly across the whole poster. They should cluster where interaction happens.

## 4. Photography language

Use premium commercial still-life photography language:

- pure white seamless backdrop;
- large softbox feel;
- soft frontal or slightly side-biased light;
- low contrast;
- clean highlights;
- subtle soft shadow under subject;
- realistic micro-texture;
- bright but not overexposed.

Material cues matter:

### Fruit

Use:

- pulp fibers;
- natural pores;
- tiny droplets;
- translucent juice;
- irregular organic texture.

### Drinks

Use:

- transparent glass;
- ice refraction;
- condensation;
- carbonation bubbles;
- translucent citrus slices.

### Ice

Use:

- transparent or semi-transparent volume;
- internal bubbles;
- melting edge detail;
- water pooling;
- clean specular highlights.

## 5. Doodle vocabulary

Useful objects:

- ladder;
- hose;
- wheelbarrow;
- fan;
- umbrella;
- spoon;
- brush;
- measuring tool;
- tiny cart;
- cloud;
- wave lines;
- motion marks;
- curved arrow.

Rule: each prop must support a micro-task.

## 6. Color system

Color hierarchy:

1. product's natural colors;
2. white background;
3. black doodle lines.

Avoid introducing unrelated accent colors.

Examples:

- watermelon: red + green + white + black line;
- peach: peach pink + cream + white + black line;
- lemon soda: pale yellow + transparent ice + white + black line.

## 7. Why the style feels premium rather than childish

The premium feeling does not come from detailed illustration. It comes from restraint:

- most of the canvas is empty;
- photography is materially convincing;
- doodles are sparse;
- typography is short;
- no decorative frame;
- no colorful background;
- no secondary marketing information;
- no excessive character design.

The playful layer works because it sits on top of a disciplined commercial-photography base.

## 8. Common failure modes

### Failure A: turns into an illustrated poster

Symptoms:

- fruit becomes painted or cartoon-like;
- doodles and product share the same illustration style.

Fix:

- reinforce realistic studio photography;
- explicitly separate “photographed hero object” from “hand-drawn overlay”.

### Failure B: turns into a cute cartoon scene

Symptoms:

- characters gain eyes, clothes, colors, expressions, or 3D bodies;
- visual focus shifts from product to mascot.

Fix:

- reduce people to black outline figures;
- remove facial and costume detail;
- keep them much smaller than the subject.

### Failure C: whitespace disappears

Symptoms:

- props fill every corner;
- extra text, badges, stars, and decorations appear.

Fix:

- state 70%–80% white negative space explicitly;
- keep only one caption and one arrow;
- remove non-functional props.

### Failure D: worker actions feel random

Symptoms:

- people wave, sit, or stand without a product-related reason.

Fix:

- map each action back to a physical or emotional product feature.

### Failure E: too many colors

Symptoms:

- pastel decorations, colored characters, or unrelated accent colors appear.

Fix:

- restrict palette to subject colors + white + black.

## 9. Reusable style sentence

Use this sentence as the conceptual anchor:

> A minimalist premium product photograph on a pure white field, transformed into a tiny narrative world through sparse, imperfect black hand-drawn workers whose actions physically express the product's features.
