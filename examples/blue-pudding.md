# Example — Blue Pudding / 清甜下午茶

## Test goal

Validate that the skill can transform a real photographed dessert into the minimal doodle poster system **without changing the product category**.

## Source subject

- object: molded blue pudding / 蓝色布丁
- form: short fluted pudding mold
- color: pale sky blue
- topping: white whipped cream + one red cherry
- container: white plate
- visual character: glossy, smooth, soft, cool, delicate

## Caption

```text
清甜下午茶
```

## Key corrections learned from testing

1. **Product identity must be locked before stylization.**  
   The blue pudding must remain pudding. It must not be reinterpreted as shaved ice, cake, or another dessert merely because the subject is blue and cool-toned.

2. **Miniature people use one fixed blank character system.**  
   They are round-headed, faceless, expressionless, without hairstyle, hats, clothing details, labels, logos, or body decoration. Their actions are communicated through pose and tools only.

3. **Handwriting is intentionally imperfect.**  
   Thin black strokes, naive and loose, slightly tilted, uneven in size and spacing, with occasional elongated horizontal strokes. Avoid standard fonts, calligraphy, rounded cute fonts, and neat typesetting.

4. **Text participates in composition.**  
   “清甜下午茶” sits in the upper-left negative space as a visual note rather than a formal headline, with a loose curved arrow connecting it to the pudding.

## Suggested miniature actions

- one figure uses a small spoon to test the soft pudding texture;
- one figure climbs a simple ladder to inspect the cherry topping;
- one figure gently wipes/polishes the glossy blue pudding surface;
- one figure carries a small ice cube or cooling tool near the plate;
- optional fifth figure checks the pudding's jiggle/bounce with a tiny measuring tool.

All actions should reference the pudding's cool, smooth, soft, sweet, and playful sensory qualities.

## Palette

```text
pale blue + cream white + cherry red + white background + black doodle lines
```

Do not add unrelated decorative colors.

## Pass criteria

- [ ] The hero object is recognizably the original blue pudding.
- [ ] The whipped cream and cherry remain visible.
- [ ] The pudding surface is smooth/glossy, not icy or granular.
- [ ] Doodle people are round-headed, blank-faced, and free of clothing/details.
- [ ] Caption reads “清甜下午茶”.
- [ ] Handwriting is loose and irregular rather than font-like.
- [ ] The composition keeps large white negative space.
- [ ] Doodle actions are product-related, not random decoration.
