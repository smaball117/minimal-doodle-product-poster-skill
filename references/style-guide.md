# Style Guide

## Style diagnosis

This is not a full illustration and not a cartoon filter. It is a staged product poster made from two layers:

1. a quiet, premium photographic object;
2. a sparse primitive black doodle story drawn around it.

The photographic object must remain the hero. The doodles act like visual annotations and miniature operators.

## Composition recipe

Default vertical poster:

- pure white or very slightly warm white background;
- approximately 70%–80% negative space;
- one hero object only;
- object placed center-lower or slightly off-center lower;
- object height usually 30%–45% of canvas height;
- title or short note usually lives in upper-left / left negative space;
- doodles cluster close to the object instead of filling the page evenly;
- one loose arrow may connect the text to the subject.

Negative space is structural. Do not fill it just because it looks empty.

## Photography language

The object should feel like clean commercial still-life photography:

- realistic material and surface texture;
- high-key soft studio light;
- low contrast;
- soft natural contact shadow;
- clean edges;
- no complex environmental background;
- preserve moisture, translucency, gloss, cream texture, bubbles, fruit flesh, fuzz, glass, metal, or other material clues when relevant.

Never convert the hero object into:

- illustration;
- clay render;
- toy;
- sticker;
- glossy 3D cartoon;
- anime or comic object.

## Doodle language

Doodles are black only and secondary to the product.

Use:

- thin to medium uneven black line;
- simple marker / pen feel;
- imperfect joins;
- slightly awkward anatomy;
- primitive tools and action marks;
- ladders, carts, hoses, spoons, umbrellas, fans, arrows, waves, clouds, droplets, motion marks when useful.

Avoid:

- polished vector icons;
- kawaii sticker characters;
- expressive mascot faces;
- colored illustration areas;
- dense comic panels;
- decorative stars or props with no story function.

## Color system

Default palette:

```text
subject-derived colors + white background + black doodles
```

Do not introduce unrelated accent colors unless the user asks for them.

## Story density

The poster should feel like a tiny working world, not a crowded scene.

- 3–5 workers is the normal range.
- 4–5 is acceptable when each job is distinct and meaningful.
- Fewer workers are better than weak workers.
- Decorative marks should be sparse.

## Text role

Text is not a conventional commercial headline block. It behaves more like a handwritten note participating in the composition.

Keep it:

- short;
- product-inspired;
- light and conversational;
- visually integrated with the object and arrow;
- subordinate to the overall quietness of the poster.

For detailed Chinese lettering rules, use `lettering-guide.md`.

## Typical failure modes

### Too much illustration

Fix: strengthen photography, reduce character detail, remove colored drawings.

### Too much decoration

Fix: delete half the secondary doodles and restore white space.

### Generic ad layout

Fix: remove grids, badges, price tags, secondary copy, logo blocks, frames, and decorative typography.

### Cute-character drift

Fix: return to the fixed faceless round-head worker system.

### Product is no longer the hero

Fix: enlarge or clarify the photographic subject and reduce doodle scale / density.
